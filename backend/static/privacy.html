Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how AI Multichannel System ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use our services through the web application, SMS, MMS, voice calls, APIs, and related features (collectively, the "Service").

By using the Service, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Service.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with the Service, we may collect:

  • Account and contact information, such as name, email address, user identifiers, and phone number.
  • Conversation content, including text messages, chat history, prompts, AI responses, and metadata such as timestamps and channel type (web, SMS, or voice).
  • SMS and MMS data, including message body, sender and recipient phone numbers, media attachments, and delivery status provided by our messaging provider.
  • Voice data, including call metadata (caller ID, called number, call duration, call status), audio recordings or transcripts when speech features are used, and text-to-speech requests.
  • Uploaded files, such as images, audio, documents, and other media you submit through the Service.
  • Technical and usage information, including IP address, browser or device type, operating system, API logs, error reports, and interaction data (for example, pages visited or features used).
  • Authentication data, such as session tokens and credentials used to access the Service.

2. How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

  • Provide, operate, maintain, and improve the Service.
  • Process and deliver messages and calls across supported channels.
  • Generate AI-powered responses using third-party model providers.
  • Convert text to speech and speech to text when voice features are enabled.
  • Store and retrieve uploaded files and conversation history.
  • Authenticate users, prevent fraud, enforce our Terms, and protect security.
  • Monitor performance, troubleshoot issues, and analyze usage trends.
  • Comply with legal obligations and respond to lawful requests.
  • Send service-related communications, including SMS replies and voice prompts.

3. AI Processing

The Service uses artificial intelligence to generate responses. Content you submit — including messages, files, and voice input — may be transmitted to third-party AI providers for processing. AI outputs may be inaccurate or incomplete and should not be relied upon as professional, medical, legal, or financial advice.

Do not submit sensitive personal information (such as health records, financial account numbers, or government identifiers) unless you accept the risk that such data may be processed by AI systems and their subprocessors.

4. SMS and Voice Communications

If you communicate with us via SMS or voice, we process your phone number and message or call content to deliver the Service. Message and data rates may apply depending on your carrier and plan.

  • We may log inbound and outbound messages and calls for delivery, support, and security purposes.
  • Voice interactions may involve recording or transcription when those features are enabled.
  • You may opt out of promotional SMS messages at any time by replying STOP. Reply HELP for assistance.
  • Opting out of promotional messages does not prevent transactional or service-related messages required to operate the Service.

5. How We Share Information

We may share information with:

  • Service providers that help us operate the Service, including:
    • Cloud hosting and infrastructure providers
    • Database, cache, and object storage providers
    • AI model providers (for example, OpenRouter and connected model hosts)
    • SMS and voice providers (for example, Twilio)
    • Text-to-speech providers (for example, ElevenLabs)
    • Speech-to-text providers
  • Legal and safety recipients when required by law, court order, or to protect rights, safety, and security.
  • Business transfers in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to this policy.

We do not sell your personal information.

We retain information for as long as necessary to provide the Service, fulfill the purposes described in this policy, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. Retention periods may vary based on data type, account status, and operational needs. You may request deletion as described below, subject to legal and operational limitations.

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to:

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@ai-multichannel-system.fly.dev. We may need to verify your identity before responding.
